📋 Hostel Rules & Information
Application Rules • Only enrolled students or college employees may apply for accommodation.
• One application per person per semester. Duplicate applications will be rejected.
• Students with disabilities receive priority consideration and ground-floor preference.
• SRC members, sponsored students, and work-for-fees students must declare their status.
• False information results in immediate disqualification and disciplinary action.

Key Collection • Keys are collected in person from the Accommodation Office, Block A, Ground Floor.
• Bring your Reference Number + Student/National ID.
• Office hours: Monday–Friday, 08:00–16:00.
• Keys are not issued to third parties under any circumstances.

🍽️ Dining Hall Hours ☀️ Breakfast: 07:00–09:00  |  🌤️ Lunch: 12:00–14:00  |  🌙 Supper: 18:00–20:00
Only residents with an active room assignment may access the dining hall. Your student card is scanned at entry.
